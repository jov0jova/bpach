Information Coefficient Analysis

Rank correlation between each indicator and forward returns. This tells you which indicators actually predict price movement.
What is IC?

IC = Spearman rank correlation between indicator value at bar N and the return N bars later. It answers: "does knowing this indicator's value tell me anything about where price will be in the future?"

IC Thresholds
|IC| > 0.05 Weak signal
|IC| > 0.10 Moderate
|IC| > 0.20 Strong (rare)
|IC| > 0.30 Very strong
ICIR

ICIR = mean(IC) / std(IC) — consistency of the signal over time. ICIR > 0.5 = reliable. ICIR > 1.0 = strong.

Path A (Entry Logic)
Use the top indicators from the IC analysis as candidates for your entry conditions. They have proven predictive power — start with |IC| > 0.05 indicators from the category that matches your trading style.
Regime-aware entries
Combine trending and ranging regime indicators: use trending-regime indicators when EMA50_slope is high, switch to ranging-regime indicators when it's low. This is the foundation of adaptive strategies.
Multi-horizon signals
If an indicator has high IC at 1-bar horizon → good for short trades. High IC at 20-bar → better for swing trades. Look for indicators that maintain IC across multiple horizons (most robust).
